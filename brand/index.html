The BytArc concept

A byte and an arc.

BytArc joins two ideas: the byte, the smallest piece of anything digital, and the arc, the path from where you start to where you land. The BytArc logo draws it: blocks of data rising in one arc, the way an idea becomes something shipped.

The mark

The BytArc mark is five blocks on a grid of 25 units: square blocks of two units and long blocks of three, stepping up to a top block and down again. Where two blocks meet, corner to corner, a soft joint links them, so the arc reads as one piece. Every corner is rounded by half a unit.

On bytarc.com the same blocks draw a picture for each kind of client: a flag for founders, a skyline for enterprises, heads round a table for executive teams, a rocket for startups, rising bars for scale-ups and a gear for operators. Scroll, and the blocks break into the 1s and 0s of a brain.

The BytArc logo in 3D

The mark is built to be solid. On bytarc.com its blocks have depth: white faces, silver sides, lit from the top left.
