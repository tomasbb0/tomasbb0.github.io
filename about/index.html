About BytArc

What is BytArc?

BytArc is an independent, founder-led AI studio in Lisbon, Portugal. It automates business processes for growing companies, advises founders and executive teams, and builds its own AI products. It was founded by Tomás Batalha.

What BytArc does

Process automation and consulting. BytArc finds the repetitive work in a growing company and automates it with AI agents, workflow automation and custom tools, and works with founders and executive teams on strategy, go-to-market and operating design. How BytArc works with companies.

Products. BytArc designs, engineers and ships AI platforms, SaaS tools and consumer apps in house: JobPilot AI, TopResume.ai, VendasIA and BytArc Education, a Portuguese-tutoring platform for young learners. It is also building OpenClaw, an agent framework for AI business automation, and runs its own operations with 11 AI agents. All BytArc products.

Who runs BytArc

BytArc was founded by Tomás Batalha. He is a former Google business analyst and Amazon business development lead, and co-founded a B2B SaaS startup where he built a €147K enterprise pipeline from zero. He holds an MSc in Strategic Management from Rotterdam School of Management and a BSc in Management from Nova SBE.

At BytArc he leads every engagement and personally designs, builds and ships every product, working with project-based collaborators and 11 AI agents. More about Tomás.

Questions about BytArc

What is BytArc?
BytArc is an independent, founder-led AI studio in Lisbon, Portugal. It automates business processes for growing companies with AI, advises founders and executive teams on strategy, go-to-market and AI adoption, and designs, builds and ships its own AI products.
What does BytArc do?
BytArc has two sides. On the consulting side it works with founders and executive teams on strategy, go-to-market and operating design. On the product side it builds AI platforms, SaaS tools and consumer apps in house, such as JobPilot AI, TopResume.ai, VendasIA and BytArc Education.
Who founded BytArc?
BytArc was founded by Tomás Batalha, a former Google business analyst and Amazon business development lead who co-founded a B2B SaaS startup. He personally designs, engineers and ships BytArc's products.
Where is BytArc based?
BytArc is based in Lisbon, Portugal. Its websites are bytarc.com in English and bytarc.pt in Portuguese.
How do you say and spell BytArc?
BytArc is said like “byte arc” and spelled B-Y-T-A-R-C, written BytArc with a capital A.
Is BytArc related to BYT-ARC welding machines, ByteArc or ByteC?
No. BytArc (bytarc.com) is an independent AI studio in Lisbon. It is not related to BYT-ARC welding machines, to ByteArc, ByteArk or ByteC, or to TARC.
