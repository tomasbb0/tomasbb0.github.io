About

Meet Burnay Labs.

Burnay Labs is a Lisbon-based holding company. Two sides: management consulting, and tech products built in house.

Consulting side: we work with founders and executive teams on strategy, go-to-market and operating design. Tech side: Tomás personally designs, engineers and ships products. AI platforms, SaaS tools, consumer apps.

Burnay Labs Education is the company's Portuguese-tutoring platform for young learners. We're also building OpenClaw, an agent framework for AI business automation.
