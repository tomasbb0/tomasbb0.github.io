AI consulting

AI consulting and process automation in Lisbon

BytArc is an independent, founder-led AI studio in Lisbon. We find the repetitive work in a growing company, automate it with AI, and ship the tools that keep it automated.

What we automate

  • Sales and outreach. Agents that research leads and write personalised B2B messages, like VendasIA, our autonomous SDR agent for Portuguese B2B sales, with an AI-assisted CRM.
  • Customer feedback. Tools that gather and analyse customer feedback and reviews at scale, built for a private client and in ALLREV.AI, our review aggregator.
  • Documents and applications. AI that writes, edits and fills in documents and forms, as in TopResume.ai and JobPilot AI, which fills in applications across 50+ hiring platforms.
  • Operations and reporting. Internal platforms, dashboards and reporting, like the operations platform we built for a private client. BytArc runs its own operations with 11 AI agents.
  • How the company really works. Company Brain, an AI interviewer that maps reporting lines, workflows and tools, starting from the CEO.

How an engagement works

  1. Diagnose. Map the process, where the time goes and what is worth automating first.
  2. Prototype. A working version on your real data, quickly, so you can judge it in use.
  3. Ship. Build it into the tools your team already uses.
  4. Hand over. Documentation and training, or we keep running and improving it.

Strategy and go-to-market

Automation pays off when it changes how a company sells and operates. BytArc also works with founders and executive teams on strategy, go-to-market and operating design, and has helped design and deliver AI programmes for executive education.
