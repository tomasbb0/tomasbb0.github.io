About BytArc

What is BytArc?

BytArc (BytArc Labs) is an AI consulting and product development company in Lisbon, Portugal, founded by Tomás Batalha. It advises founders and executive teams, and it designs, builds and ships its own AI products.

What BytArc does

Consulting. BytArc works with founders and executive teams on strategy, go-to-market and operating design, and on putting AI to work in their business.

Products. BytArc designs, engineers and ships AI platforms, SaaS tools and consumer apps in house: JobPilot AI, TopResume.ai, VendasIA and BytArc Education, a Portuguese-tutoring platform for young learners. It is also building OpenClaw, an agent framework for AI business automation, and runs its own operations with 11 AI agents.

Who runs BytArc

BytArc was founded by Tomás Batalha. He is a former Google business analyst and Amazon business development lead, and co-founded a B2B SaaS startup where he built a €147K enterprise pipeline from zero. He holds an MSc in Strategic Management from Rotterdam School of Management and a BSc in Management from Nova SBE.

At BytArc he leads the consulting work and personally designs, builds and ships every product.

Questions about BytArc

What is BytArc?
BytArc (BytArc Labs) is an AI consulting and product development company based in Lisbon, Portugal. It advises founders and executive teams on strategy, go-to-market and AI adoption, and it designs, builds and ships its own AI software products.
What does BytArc do?
BytArc has two sides. On the consulting side it works with founders and executive teams on strategy, go-to-market and operating design. On the product side it builds AI platforms, SaaS tools and consumer apps in house, such as JobPilot AI, TopResume.ai, VendasIA and BytArc Education.
Who founded BytArc?
BytArc was founded by Tomás Batalha, a former Google business analyst and Amazon business development lead who co-founded a B2B SaaS startup. He personally designs, engineers and ships BytArc's products.
Where is BytArc based?
BytArc is based in Lisbon, Portugal. Its websites are bytarc.com in English and bytarc.pt in Portuguese.
How do you say and spell BytArc?
BytArc is said like “byte arc” and spelled B-Y-T-A-R-C, written BytArc with a capital A.
Is BytArc related to BYT-ARC welding machines, ByteArc or ByteC?
No. BytArc (BytArc Labs, bytarc.com) is an AI consulting and product development company in Lisbon. It is not related to BYT-ARC welding machines, to ByteArc, ByteArk or ByteC, or to TARC.
